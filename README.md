## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Tukoni: Forest Keepers**. This trainer enhances exploration and collection in Tukoni: Forest Keepers with adjustable movement speed, inventory assistance, recipe and herb management, and convenient progress options for its handcrafted puzzles and tea-brewing activities.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed way to explore the forest, collect herbs, prepare tea, revisit locations, and enjoy the game's puzzles without repetitive gathering or extended backtracking.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Start the game and load a save before activating movement options. Enable the movement speed function only after Traveller appears in the world. Use the multiplier carefully in narrow areas and disable it before interacting with objects or entering transitions.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the inventory once so the trainer can detect supported herbs, ingredients, and collected items. Activate the inventory option, select the desired item in the game, and change its quantity through the trainer. Avoid editing unidentified entries and save normally after confirming the result.

### World & Environment

Explore the game's four hand-drawn locations, speak with forest residents, follow hidden paths, gather herbs, discover cozy spots, and use collected materials for tea recipes. Trainer options are intended to reduce repeated collection and travel while leaving the exploration and puzzle structure intact.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no combat in Tukoni: Forest Keepers. The game focuses on exploration, conversations, item collection, tea preparation, and handcrafted environmental puzzles, so the trainer provides no health, damage, ammunition, or enemy-related options.
